How we think

Most AI consulting fails in one of two ways. Either it stays at the strategic level, a deck full of frameworks that doesn't change what anyone does on Monday morning. Or it jumps straight to tools, deploying Copilot or a chatbot before anyone has asked whether it fits the work.

We work in the middle. We start by mapping how work flows through your org today, score each stage against the five patterns where AI delivers real enterprise value (creativity, analysis, strategy, research, automation), and come back with a ranked list of opportunities. Then we pilot the top ones in a measured, reversible way, before scaling.

This is the approach that worked inside Google. It works because it treats AI adoption as a workflow design problem, not a tooling problem.
