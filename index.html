How we think

Most AI consulting fails in one of two ways. Either it stays at the strategic level, a deck full of frameworks that doesn't change what anyone does day to day. Or it introduces tools before anyone has asked how they will be used, and whether it fits the work.

We work in the middle. We start by mapping how work flows through your org today, score each stage against the five patterns where AI delivers real enterprise value (creativity, analysis, strategy, research, automation), and generate a list of opportunities. Then we pilot a select few in a measured, reversible way, before scaling.

This is the approach that worked inside Google. It works because it treats AI adoption as a workflow design problem, not a tooling problem.
